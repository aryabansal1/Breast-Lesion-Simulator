# Tissue electrical properties: sources, adoption and caveats

All material models live in `data/materials/library.yaml` (generated by `scripts/build_library.py`; its
SHA-256 is written to every run's `metadata.json`). Each entry carries a `provenance` block (source,
DOI, table, frequency range, temperature, in-vivo/ex-vivo, geometry, n, data type) and `valid_range_hz`.
Outside `valid_range_hz` the value is **extrapolated** and flagged (`materials.extrapolation: warn|error|allow`).

The solver uses complex admittivity \(\gamma(f)=\sigma(f)+j\omega\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r(f)\).
Models: `gabriel_cole_cole` (IT'IS), `sigma_const_eps_gabriel` (IT'IS LF conductivity + Gabriel
permittivity *without* the Gabriel ionic term, so the loss is not counted twice), `cole_impedivity`
(UCI-derived, \(\rho^*=\rho_\infty+(\rho_0-\rho_\infty)/(1+(jf/f_c)^{1-\alpha})\), \(\gamma=1/\rho^*\)),
`cole_cole_single` (Surowiec), `constant`, `tabulated`.

## Default assignment (`uci_breast`)

| Label | Tissue | Material | Data type | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | fat | `adipose_uci` | fitted to derived features | UCI `I0` interpreted as Ω·cm (dataset says "ohm"); see below |
| 29, 95, 125, 250 | glandular, TDLU, duct, calcification | `glandular_uci` | fitted | TDLU/duct/calcification have no breast-specific data; mapped to glandular (assumption) |
| 2, 33 | skin, nipple | `skin_itis_lf` | IT'IS measured/compiled | wet vs dry skin uncertain; nipple = skin (assumption) |
| 40, 41 | muscle, assumed chest-wall block | `muscle_itis_lf` | IT'IS | anisotropy ignored |
| 88 | Cooper's ligament | `connective_itis_lf` | IT'IS | |
| 150, 225 | artery, vein | `blood_itis_lf` | IT'IS | vessels at 0.5 mm are partly resolved only |
| 200 | tumour | `carcinoma_uci` (override with `tumour.material`) | fitted | see alternatives below |

Alternative assignment `itis_only` uses IT'IS breast fat / gland for 1 / 29; note **IT'IS "Breast Gland"
low-frequency conductivity is a thyroid-gland substitute** in V5.0.

## Source-by-source review

| Source | What is reported | Adopted? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jossinet 1998 (Physiol Meas 19:61, doi:10.1088/0967-3334/19/1/006) | 120 impedivity spectra, 64 patients, 488 Hz–1 MHz, ex vivo | Indirectly, via UCI features | The PDF and tables were not provided |
| Estrela da Silva, Marques de Sá, Jossinet, Med Biol Eng Comput (doi:10.1007/BF02344684) — `BF02344684.pdf` | Definitions of the UCI spectral features (I0 = low-frequency impedivity limit, PA500, DA, DR, Max IP...) | Yes (feature definitions) | Basis of `scripts/fit_uci_cole.py`; the fit is **inferred** (3 Cole parameters from 5 features, 106/106 samples RMS residual < 0.05) |
| Surowiec et al. 1988 (IEEE Trans Biomed Eng 35(4), Table I on p. 262) — `Dielectric_properties_of_breast_carcinoma_and_the_surrounding_tissues.pdf` | Table I: εs, ε∞, τ, α, σDC per sample, 28 samples / 7 specimens, 20 kHz–100 MHz, 37 °C, ex vivo | Yes | `carcinoma_surowiec` = medians of samples with σDC > 1.05 mS/cm, `peritumoral_low_surowiec` = σDC ≤ 1.05 mS/cm. **Our grouping**; fit valid 100 kHz–100 MHz, so < 100 kHz is extrapolated and flagged |
| Halter et al. 2009 (Physiol Meas 30(6), doi:10.1088/0967-3334/30/6/S08, PMC2792899) — `nihms-160775.pdf` | Probe EIS 10 kHz–10 MHz, in vivo vs ex vivo; IDCa/DCIS σ(10 kHz) 0.145 ± 0.012 S/m | Partly | `carcinoma_halter` single-frequency σ; ε not adopted (probe-derived permittivity at 500 Hz is dominated by electrode polarisation). Main use: in-vivo values higher than ex-vivo → justifies sensitivity ranges |
| Chauveau et al. 1999 (Ann NY Acad Sci 873:42, doi:10.1111/j.1749-6632.1999.tb09447.x) — `PDF document.pdf` (scanned; OCR) | R–S–Z_CPE equivalent-circuit parameters in **ohms** for a probe | No | Probe/geometry-specific impedance; no cell constant to convert to σ/ε. Ohms must not be assigned to a 3-D region |
| Gregory et al. 2012, "The Cole relaxation frequency as a parameter to identify cancer in breast tissue" (Med Phys, doi:10.1118/1.4725172) — `PDF document 2.pdf` | Cole fc and α per sample from probe impedance | No | Only shape parameters; no absolute σ/ε |
| Du et al. 2017 (Medicine, doi:10.1097/MD.0000000000005970, PMC5287972) — `PDF document 3.pdf` | Cole R0, R∞, fc, α of excised tissue | No | Authors state R0/R∞ depend on electrode geometry and tissue volume |
| Meani et al. 2023 (Ann Biomed Eng, doi:10.1007/s10439-023-03159-4) — `PDF document 4.pdf` | Needle four-electrode probe, 1–100 kHz, modulus/phase and ROC AUCs | No | No cell constant / σ values; diagnostic statistics are out of scope |
| Saleh & Mathloom 2025 (Rev Fac Cienc 14(2):6–15, doi:10.15446/rev.fac.cienc.v14n2.117892) — `PDF document 6.pdf` | LCR-meter impedance, conductance, "conductivity ×10⁻⁶", permittivity, 1–300 kHz | No | Table 2 mislabelled ("thyroid"), units ambiguous, control impedance 57.6 MΩ / σ ≈ 6×10⁻⁷ S/m are physically implausible for soft tissue |
| Lazebnik et al. 2007 (Phys Med Biol 52(20), doi:10.1088/0031-9155/52/20/002) — `PDF document 5.pdf` | 0.5–20 GHz | No | Microwave Cole–Cole fits must not be extrapolated to kHz |
| IT'IS V5.0 | Gabriel parameters, LF conductivity | Yes, normal tissues | Fat LF σ is from one sample; gland LF row is a thyroid substitute |
| UCI Breast Tissue | 9 derived features per spectrum | Yes (via fit) | Derived, not raw spectra |

## Known disagreements (treat as uncertainty, not as truth)

Carcinoma conductivity near 100 kHz: UCI-fit ≈ 0.30 S/m, Surowiec median ≈ 0.51 S/m, Halter (10 kHz,
probe, ex vivo) 0.145 S/m. Use `configs/victre_dense_sensitivity.yaml` to see how this propagates.
